# Recycling Concepts

## Steps For Recycling

### Collecting
 - Set out bins around the school for janitors
 - Students to collect trash in to be sent to the recycling station
 - Will be collected during class time given

### Washing
 -  Washing basin
 -  Relatively pressured water
 -  washing machine-esque design

### Sorting
 - AI sorting through trap doors
 - Will be dropped into bins for further use
 - Bins will not have conveyor belts

### Shredding
 -  Sorted bins will be shredded based on plastic type
 -  Shredded pellets will be stored in a bin under the shredder

### Melting
 - Pellets will be dumped into a melder
 - Melted into 3d printer filament

### Shaping
 - Filament will be wrapped around spools directly after coming out of the melter
